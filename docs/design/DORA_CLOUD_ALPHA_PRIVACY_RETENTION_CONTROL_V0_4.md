# OD-11C-24 — INTERNAL ALPHA DATA SUBJECT BOUNDARY

For CLOSED_INTERNAL_ALPHA:

allowed real-person audio is restricted to:

- Project Owner;
- explicitly invited internal Alpha participants who knowingly participate in the Alpha and explicitly opt in to the recording/Cloud-processing test.

Out of scope before external legal review:

- customer meetings;
- production business calls;
- incidental recording of unrelated persons;
- recordings where a participant has not agreed to the Alpha test;
- public users;
- anonymous users;
- hidden/passive recording;
- systematic employee surveillance;
- call recording beyond existing mic-only explicit-start scope.

Synthetic/test fixtures remain allowed under their existing data authority.

This is a hard Alpha scope boundary, not merely disclosure text.
