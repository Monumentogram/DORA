# Separate POCO M5 physical Recovery scope

The owner authorized an exact POCO M5 physical profile, three substantive preflights
and six synthetic scenarios: normal completion/recovery/replay, K08 and K12 for
STREAM and MICROFILE. This is separate from completed E36 and API33 evidence.

App source remains `79d930d73ce7836c3cf5bec10be85f800936e829`; app SHA-256 remains
`9dd8f1dfd05ad4404e9c52ad5e7b11b575e6ba03bcba87a176bd953ddc649a7c`.
Only Android tests/helpers and host admission/launcher/runner may change. A new test
APK must instrument the original package with a compatible certificate. Source,
test artifact, actual installed hashes and private physical identity are separately
bound; prior emulator profiles and admission remain unchanged.

The physical route accepts only its explicit profile and measured exact identity.
The host binds serial, model, firmware, API, ABI, product, build type and SELinux.
Unknown devices, unknown installed package provenance, skipped instrumentation,
incomplete retention and unverified cleanup cannot receive credit.

Before any hard-kill scenario, one separate bounded synthetic probe may determine
whether stock non-root external SIGKILL works against the target app UID and SELinux
context. It is not a campaign result. Root installation, bootloader unlocking,
SELinux changes, force-stop and self-kill substitutes are excluded. A missing valid
controller blocks the four kill rows only. The three preflights and two normal
flows retain independent eligibility.

The exact inherited K12 recipes remain distinct from K08: STREAM persistence uses
the v0.7 seed inherited by v0.8, while MICROFILE uses its v0.3 quarantine seed.
Original real Keystore/SQLite/Tink, fixtures, independent byte oracle, replay,
C/A/R semantics and loss limits remain binding. No product fix is part of this task.

Execution requires reviewed source and private packet, required local checks and
exact-source CI. Physical execution and outcomes are reported separately after
measurement. No result or readiness is claimed by this scope document.

Prior E36 and API33 campaigns are not automatically rerun. Their histories remain
on their accepted versions. Full 0D.6 remains OPEN, PR86 stays draft and unmerged;
full D1/D2/D5, other phones, old Android versions and endurance remain outside scope.
